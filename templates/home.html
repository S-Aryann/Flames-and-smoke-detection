Flame and Smoke Detection Algorithm Based on ODConvBS-YOLOv5s

ABOUT

Real-time and accurate detection of flame and smoke is an important prerequisite to reduce the loss caused by fire. There are exists some problems in traditional detection algorithms of flame and smoke, such as low accuracy, high miss rate, low detection efficiency, and low detection rate of small targets

Fire will inflict significant damage to human life and property in daily activities, as well as do harm to the healthy development of society
